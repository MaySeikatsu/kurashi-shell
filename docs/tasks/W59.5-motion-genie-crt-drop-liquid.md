## Build
- `genie`: mesh 2 × 48 (rows across the funnel). Phase 1 (p 1 → 0.45 closing): the far edge narrows toward the origin's
  width along a smoothstep curve, rows bend toward the origin's centre; phase 2: the whole body slides into the origin.
  Open is the reverse (pours out of the lamp). Any edge; origin = the module's rect (dock icon, bar module, pill).
- `crt`: fragment pass on the captured card + scale: on = dot (5 % of p) → horizontal line (to 40 %) → full height
  (to 100 %), bloom on the bright line, slight barrel, scanlines fading out by p = 1; off = the reverse with a 120 ms
  afterglow dot. Flash-safe (brief §11): luminance step ≤ 20 %, one per transition; under `flash` restrictions it falls
  back to `fade`.
- `drop`: a drop (r 6 px, the look's ink/accent role) leaves the origin on the `drop` curve to the card's centre; on
  impact an iris reveal with a wavefront ring (2–4 px) that refracts the card beneath (fragment displacement along the
  ring's normal); close: the card contracts to a drop that rises home, or evaporates (toasts).
- `liquid`: the morph starts from the pressed module's own rect and radius (IslandGeom), the neck is a smooth-min union
  that stretches and necks off once the card stands free (or stays joined, per D2), width leads, height lags; content
  cross-fades. Retarget morphs.
