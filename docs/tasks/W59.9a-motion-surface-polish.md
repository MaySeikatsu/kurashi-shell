## Why
W59.3 wired the card surfaces (island, panel, launcher, centre, dialog) and left four gaps (its report, rows 658/659):
- `liquid` (W59.5 drafted `forms/Liquid.qml` against ShapeForm) never ran: `SurfaceForm.shapes` must list it, MorphShape
  must read `shape.neck`, and the origin's radius (`fromR`) must come from IslandGeom.
- toast, dock, sidebar (and peek) don't take the shape forms (expand, iris, stamp, outline): wrap their cards in
  `ShapeLayer { run; edge }` and widen those rows' `surfaces` in `crates/kurashi-motion/src/forms.rs`; toasts' OPEN still
  takes the drip even under a sheet form — a sheet form must run both ways.
- iris starts from the origin's centre, not where the user pressed (row 658): carry the press point (bar modules already
  note a press — `module:<id>`, I340) to FormHost as `pressAt` and use it for iris (and drop's bead).
- Visible seams at landing: under a sheet form on a morph, the captured card shows its own rounded top corners and
  border, and the join's fillets return only over the last 15 % — the corners visibly change as it lands; on the
  sidebar the fillets are inside the captured column and warp with it. The landing must be seamless: the captured card
  takes the joined shape (square top where it meets the bar, fillets drawn by the surface outside the capture, cross-faded
  so p = 1 is exactly the rest frame with no corner pop).
