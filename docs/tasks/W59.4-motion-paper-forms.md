# W59.4 — sheet forms, paper: fold (origami) · roll (+scroll) · book · crumple

Brief `docs/ux/motion-engine.md` §5 (sheet family, the table rows), §10, §11; contract `W59-motion-engine.md`
("SheetForm"); K83 + D2. Common rules `docs/tasks/README.md`. Model: Opus. Reviewed frame by frame on niri.

## Goal
The user's paper forms at a polished, physical quality: paper has thickness of light (crease highlights and shadows),
panels unfold with a lag that reads as hand-made, the roll shows the back of the paper, a crumple looks like paper and not
noise. Kami's origami is the reference piece of this whole engine.

## Owned paths
`frontend/components/motion/forms/{SheetForm,Fold,Roll,Book,Crumple}.qml` · `frontend/shaders/motion/{fold,roll,book,
crumple}.{vert,frag}` (+ `.qsb`) · their rows in `frontend/test-motion.sh` · `motion/origami.toml` draft (finalised in W59.9).

## Build
- `SheetForm.qml` (the base every sheet form uses): `ShaderEffectSource` of the surface's card (fill, material, border,
  content; `hideSource` while busy; `live` while busy; texture at device pixels), `ShaderEffect` with `mesh: GridMesh`,
  hinge = the card's edge on the bar (or the origin's edge), signals the host to fade the elevation shadow out at
  p < 0.1 and in at rest, to draw a translucent fill solid while warped and restore the blur region at rest, the join's
  fillets fading in over the last 15 %; created/released log lines; destroyed at rest (PSS back to baseline).
- `fold`: accordion of N panels (auto: one per ~120 px along the axis away from the hinge, 2–5); panel k's angle from
  its own sub-progress (lag 0.12), perspective from a fixed focal length (no projection matrix tricks: compute the
  projected vertex in the shader), crease shading from the panel normal against `Theme`'s light direction (the K82
  light), a 1-px crease line; `axes = 2`: a small square unfolds across, then along (letter fold). Close = reverse.
- `roll`: a cylinder radius r at the roll front; vertices beyond the front wrap onto it; back faces show the
  `backside` role (default: the card's fill darkened 8 %), self-shadow under the roll; `scroll` variant: two rollers
  from the centre outward (makimono).
- `book`: spine auto (perpendicular to the bar through the card's centre) ; closed = one half with the cover tone; the
  moving half rotates 180° with a slight curl (bend at its free edge); landing settles flat.
- `crumple`: per-vertex hashed displacement growing with p, mesh contracting to the centroid, per-cell rotation, crease
  normals from a noise field for shading; ball end state ~18 % of the card's shorter side, then it drops into the origin
  (toast: flicks off with the toast's dismiss direction). Open = uncrumple, wrinkles last.
- Stepped/pixel looks: the curve steps; at rest exact.

## Acceptance
Scrubbed niri shots p = 0.1/0.3/0.5/0.7/0.9/1 for each form on panel, launcher, sidebar (roll), toast (crumple) —
kami, washi, sekai, glass (translucent), light/dark, 1× and 1.5×; p = 1 equals motion-off; reversal at 0.5. Bench
(`tools/bench/motion.sh` if W59.9 hasn't landed: the W33 harness) on A and B's iGPU: CPU p95 ≤ 8 ms, GPU ≤ 4 ms at 1×,
PSS ≤ +12 MiB while moving and back within 2 s; idle 0 frames, no layer at rest. The lead reviews every shot sheet.
