# W59.3 — every surface class through FormHost; shape forms expand · iris · stamp · outline

Brief `docs/ux/motion-engine.md` §5 (shape family), §6, §7, §10; contract `W59-motion-engine.md`; K83 with the user's
D2 (hinge) and D3 (island). Common rules `docs/tasks/README.md`. Model: Opus. Reviewed on niri shots.

## Goal
Each of the 12 surface classes resolves its form from `motion.form.<surface>.<dir>` and its origin, and draws today's
form unchanged by default. The four new shape forms work on every class their meta allows.

## Owned paths
Motion parts of `frontend/surfaces/{Stage,Bar,Dock,EdgeModules,Toasts,PolkitDialog,Sidebar*,Osd*}.qml`,
`frontend/components/launcher/BarMorph.qml`, the tray/context menu hosts, `frontend/components/island/{MorphShape,
IslandGeom}.qml` (origin rects, clip shapes) · `frontend/components/motion/forms/{Expand,Iris,Stamp,Outline}.qml` ·
`frontend/test-motion.sh` (the surface matrix) — coordinate with W59.6 only through the contract.

## Build
- A `FormHost` (or the morph's form input) per surface with `surface:` its class id; the origin from IslandGeom
  (`module:<id>`, `sidebar:<side>`, the pill), else the bar's centre, else the screen edge (brief §7).
- Shape forms in the morph: `expand` (clip rect from the origin edge), `iris` (circle clip from the origin/press point;
  the join's fillets appear as the circle crosses the bar's edge), `stamp` (scale 1.06 → 1 with a contact squash, the
  elevation shadow tightening from its level + 1), `outline` (6–8 stepped outline rects from origin to the card in the
  look's outline role, then the card at once; content none until then).
- Interrupt and retarget rules (brief §7): reversal continues the form in flight; retarget between panels morphs.
- The island (D3): its open/close (pill → card) runs every form with the pill as origin and hinge, following the
  default unless `theme.motion.form.island.follow` is off; face changes and peeks keep K42's springs; the polkit dialog keeps
  its focus rules; toasts keep the drip for `grow`.

## Acceptance

allows, scrubbed shots at 0.15/0.5/0.85/1 (top and bottom bar; washi, sekai, kami; light and dark); p = 1 equals the
motion-off frame for every surface; a reversal at p = 0.5 and a quick → session retarget mid-way. `just suites` (merge
